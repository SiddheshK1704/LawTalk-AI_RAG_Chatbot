02 — Product

A workspace built around the source text.

Your conversation sits in the centre. For legal questions, the passages LawTalk retrieved sit beside it, so every answer can be read against its source.

03 — How it works

Three steps from question to source.

  1. 01

    Ask

    Submit a legal question in plain language, for example “When can the police arrest without a warrant?”

  2. 02

    Retrieve

    LawTalk finds the most relevant passages in its legal knowledge base, by meaning and by the exact words and section numbers you use.

  3. 03

    Verify

    The answer is written from those passages only. Each claim carries a citation that opens the evidence behind it.

04 — Features

What it does, and where it stops.

  1. A

    Legal document retrieval

    Searches the indexed statutes by meaning and by exact terms. A question naming a provision, such as “Article 21” or “Section 438 CrPC”, goes straight to it.

  2. B

    Evidence-grounded answers

    Legal answers are written only from the retrieved passages. If those passages don’t cover the question, LawTalk says so instead of guessing.

  3. C

    Source citations

    Every cited passage opens in the evidence panel with its document, provision and page, wherever the source records them.

  4. D

    General mode for everything else

    Questions that aren’t legal get a normal assistant answer, with no document search and no evidence panel, so it is always clear which answers are grounded in sources.

Indexed documents

Constitution of India · Indian Penal Code · Code of Criminal Procedure · Indian Evidence Act · Information Technology Act · Motor Vehicles Act · Consumer Protection Act, 2019 · POCSO Act · labour-law study material (ICSI).

The IPC, CrPC and Evidence Act were replaced by the BNS, BNSS and BSA from 1 July 2024; their passages carry that note. Coverage is limited to these documents.
