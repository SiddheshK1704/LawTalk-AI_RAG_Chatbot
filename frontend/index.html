What LawTalk does

  1. 01

    Answers you can check

    Each statement carries a numbered citation. Click 1 and the passage it came from opens beside the answer.

  2. 02

    The evidence, not a summary of it

    The evidence panel shows the retrieved text itself, with the document title and page number when the source records one.

  3. 03

    Honest about its limits

    If the indexed documents don't cover your question, LawTalk says so rather than guessing at sections or penalties.

  4. 04

    Your research, kept

    Conversations are saved to your account and searchable, so you can return to a line of inquiry later.

How it works

Three steps from question to source.

  1. 1

    Ask

    Write your question in plain language, for example “When can police arrest without a warrant?”

  2. 2

    Retrieve

    LawTalk searches the indexed documents and selects the five most relevant passages.

  3. 3

    Read the cited answer

    The answer streams in, written only from those passages, each claim linked to its source.
